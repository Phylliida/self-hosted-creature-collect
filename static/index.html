Welcome!

Step 1 of 2: download app data — fonts, POI icons, low-zoom world map, and creature sprites + species info (~300 MB total). Without this the map shows no labels and wild creatures show as red dots.

Step 2 of 2: download map data for your area. Pan and zoom the map to your region, then tap the ↓ save current view button at the bottom of the screen. This grabs offline tiles, POIs, walking paths, and transit so the app works without internet. You can repeat this for any region you visit.
